Our 3-Phase Internship Methodology

lightbulb

Phase 1: Discovery

Students are introduced to different engineering disciplines, basic coding/design principles, and industry tools through workshops and short, exploratory projects. No prior experience expected!

code

Phase 2: Application

Students work in teams on guided projects, focusing on practical problem-solving, Git, Agile methodologies, and creating a portfolio-ready project.

verified

Phase 3: Mastery

Participants join advanced tracks (AI/ML, Cybersecurity, Renewables) and tackle complex problems with industry mentors to become truly job-ready.
